Vint

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Vint is a card game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: Vint does not collect any data about you.

Data we collect

None. Vint has no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. The app does not send your data to us or to any third party. You can play Vint fully offline.

Data stored on your device

Vint keeps a few things on your device so that the game works:

  • your settings (language, sound, card deck, help level);
  • your progress (completed lessons, Daily Deal results and streak);
  • your game statistics;
  • the game in progress, so you can continue it later.

This data stays on your device. We cannot see it. If you delete the app, iOS deletes this data too.

Crash reports

If you turned on "Share With App Developers" in iOS settings, Apple can send us anonymous crash reports and usage figures. Apple controls this, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Vint collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future version of Vint adds a feature that uses data (for example, online play), we will update this page before that version is released.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the support page.
